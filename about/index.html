About Reinhard Moosauer & m1b.de

Software Developer, System Architect, and Expert in Application Development & Industrial Automation.

Moosauer IT Consulting (2000 – 2013)

Under the name Moosauer IT Beratung (Moosauer IT Consulting), I worked independently from 2000 to 2013 across a variety of IT projects, software architecture, and client consulting engagements. Key focus areas included developing tailored enterprise solutions, database architectures, and system integrations.
